Cluster infrastructure installed.

Wait for all pods to be ready before proceeding:

  kubectl get pods -A

Wait for CNPG CRDs to be registered:

  kubectl wait --for=condition=established \
    crd/clusters.postgresql.cnpg.io --timeout=60s

Wait for MongoDB Community CRDs to be registered:

  kubectl wait --for=condition=established \
    crd/mongodbcommunity.mongodbcommunity.mongodb.com --timeout=60s
